Here are the actors and their user-goal use cases:

**Visitors**

1. Browse a comprehensive list of hiking trails.
2. Filter hiking trails based on specific criteria (difficulty, length, starting point).
3. View detailed descriptions of hiking trails.
4. Create a user account to access personalized recommendations.
5. Record fitness parameters to receive trail suggestions.
6. Record progress during a hike by marking reference points.
7. Share live location through a broadcasting URL.
8. Plan group hikes, add group members, and confirm participation.
9. Start, terminate, and track hikes with notifications for unfinished hikes or late group members.

**Local Guides**

1. Add detailed descriptions of hikes.
2. Add parking facilities information.
3. Link parking lots and huts to specific trails as starting or arrival points.
4. Upload pictures of huts and connect these locations directly to hikes.

**Platform Managers**

1. Verify new hut worker registrations.
2. Broadcast weather alerts for specific areas through push notifications.
3. Maintain an organized and secure user system.
4. Facilitate collaboration between local guides, hut workers, and visitors.

**Hut Workers**

1. Register and be verified to access the platform.
2. Add or update information about assigned huts, including uploading pictures and describing facilities.
3. Monitor and report on the condition of nearby trails.
4. Provide situational updates for hikers, such as severe weather or physical obstructions.
